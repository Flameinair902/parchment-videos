## Do
- Put a **concrete number in the hook line or first screen** (Tea Race, DNA cost, moons, carton, Panama, Sun/Moon, pencil).
- **Contradict or flip two familiar things**: lowest skip, most YT breakouts.
- Cosmic: compare **familiar bodies** (Sun, Moon, Mercury) rather than obscure ones (Betelgeuse 5 YT views).
- Hooks with **stakes or a contest plus a number** (Tea Race, DNA cost).
- First visible change within 1 s; **hold the twist to 6-9 s**, not the first line (Cleopatra vs moons/carton/Panama).
- Keep videos **21-24 s** and ~55-65 words.
- YouTube titles: a short curiosity line with a number or a flip ("2 moons are bigger than a planet", "Your lead pencil has 0 lead").
- Exploded only at under 24 s with a contradiction hook (carton, pencil vs smoke alarm/card).

## Avoid
- Hooks built on one obscure subject (named bird, sailor, turtle, general): skip 0.51-0.62.
- Warning or danger framing about the home (skip 0.75).
- Videos of 25-30 s with 80+ words (smoke alarm, card antenna).
- Spending experiments on comment prompts (5 tries, ~0 interactions). Keep one short open question and move on.

## Watching (not a rule yet)
- Myth-busting angle: bacteria (IG weak 115/0.60, YT 257) vs pencil (YT 707 in 6 h). Great Wall (lowpoly) tests it on a map. Mixed so far.
- Data style on everyday/body or history topics: 2 weak IG posts (Cleopatra, bacteria). One more weak one would make it an Avoid.
- Morning post (Tea Race, ~09:00 UK): one data point; runs can't change post time.
- Payoff spoken in the hook line (Cleopatra): one weak post; looks negative.
