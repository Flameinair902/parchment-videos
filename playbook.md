## Do
- Put a **concrete number in the hook line or first screen** (Tea Race, DNA cost, moons, carton, Panama, Sun/Moon).
- **Contradict or flip two familiar things** (moons vs Mercury, Sun vs Moon, Panama, carton): lowest skip, most YT breakouts.
- Cosmic: compare **familiar bodies** (Sun, Moon, Mercury) rather than obscure ones (Betelgeuse 5 YT views).
- Hooks with **stakes or a contest plus a number** (Tea Race, DNA cost).
- First visible change within 1 s; **hold the "why"/twist to 6-9 s**, not the first line (Cleopatra vs moons/carton/Panama).
- Keep videos **21-24 s** and ~55-65 words.
- YouTube titles: a short curiosity line with a number or a flip ("2 moons are bigger than a planet").
- Exploded only at under 24 s with a contradiction hook (carton vs smoke alarm/card).

## Avoid
- Hooks built on one obscure subject (named bird, sailor, turtle, general): skip 0.51-0.62.
- Warning or danger framing about the home (skip 0.75).
- Videos of 25-30 s with 80+ words (smoke alarm, card antenna).
- Spending experiments on comment prompts (4 tries, ~0 interactions). Keep one short open question and move on.

## Watching (not a rule yet)
