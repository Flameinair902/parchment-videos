## Do
- Put a **concrete number in the hook line, title or first screen**.
- **Contradict or flip two familiar things**: lowest skip, most YT breakouts.
- Cosmic: compare **familiar bodies** (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Earth, Pluto, Jupiter).
- First visible change within 1 s; hold the twist to 6-9 s.
- Keep videos **21-24 s** and ~55-65 words.
- YouTube titles: a short curiosity line with a number or a flip.
- Exploded only at under 24 s with a contradiction hook.
- Myth-bust a familiar name/belief in cosmic or exploded.
- Lowpoly: draw each route while its line is spoken (sequential routes).

## Avoid
- Hooks built on one obscure subject (named bird, sailor, turtle, general): skip 0.51-0.62.
- Warning or danger framing about the home (skip 0.75).
- Videos of 25-30 s with 80+ words.
- Myth-bust framing in data or lowpoly (bacteria 284, Great Wall 330).
- Race / stakes hooks outside the clipper-race story (railroad, Nellie Bly both below median). Moved from Watching.
- Spending experiments on comment prompts. Keep one short open question.
